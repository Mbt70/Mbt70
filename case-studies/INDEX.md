## Case study format

A strong case study should answer five questions:

1. **Problem** — What was the real bottleneck or decision problem?
2. **Role** — What did I personally design, manage, build, or verify?
3. **Artifact** — What concrete thing was produced?
4. **Evidence** — What shows the work became testable, reviewable, or useful?
5. **Reflection** — What changed, what was learned, and what comes next?

---

## Publishing guardrails

Before publishing a new case study, check:

- no private messages, channel IDs, credentials, or personal internal notes
- no team member names or organization details unless explicitly approved
- no competition/private-repo material that should remain confidential
- screenshots are sanitized and useful without exposing sensitive context
- claims are grounded in artifacts, tests, screenshots, logs, or submitted documents
